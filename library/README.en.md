## Core Visual Methods (`M-001` – `M-042`)

- `M-001`: **In-Scale Reference Framing** — Pair products with familiar size anchors (hands, human models, cupcakes, 19-inch racks) so buyers grasp physical scale without mental unit conversion.
- `M-002`: **Descriptive Callout Lines & Mobile Text Guard** — Bridge the gap between skipped text and ambiguous close-ups using leader lines and 2–6 word callouts while protecting `375px` mobile legibility.
- `M-003`: **Included Accessories Knolling & Optional Isolation** — Show all bundled items in an `"Included in the Box"` gallery image and label optional add-ons with explicit `"Sold Separately"` microcopy.
- `M-004`: **Product-in-Action Hero & 5-Element Above-the-Fold Loop** — *"Don't show me fancy illustrations. Show me your product. Or even better, your product in action."*
- `M-005`: **Single-Column Semantic Spec Grouping & Alternating Row Shading** — Break up `"walls of specs"` into 5–8 row semantic subsections with alternating row shading and a top `"Key Specs"` summary.
- `M-006`: **Expanded Vertical Sections + Sticky ToC over Horizontal Tabs** — Replace desktop horizontal tabs with vertically `"Expanded Sections"` + a `"Sticky ToC"` bar so engineers and buyers never miss hidden specs or CAD files.
- `M-007`: **Weight/Color Contrast Hierarchy, Same-Hue Dark-Bg Text & Borderless Grouping** — *"Size isn't everything"* (`2` font weights + `3` color shades), *"Don't use grey text on colored backgrounds"*, and *"Use fewer borders"*.
- `M-008`: **Action Pyramid Button Styling & Contained Small Icons** — *"Semantics are secondary"* (`1` solid Primary CTA per view) and *"Everything has an intended size"* (`16–24px` icons inside `48×48px` containers).
- `M-009`: **Multi-Deliverable Visual Bundling & 2:1 Target Tier Framing** — *"Escape the context of an e-book"* and give `"The Complete Package"` *"twice as much screen space"* (`78%` chose the `$149` tier).
- `M-010`: **Distinctive Visual Asset Guard & Zero-Thought Concrete Metaphor** — Guard instant-recognition cues (`"A straw in an orange"`, horizontal wordmarks, Angostura's oversized label) so buyers never think: *"I don't want to think to buy orange juice."*
- `M-011`: **Proximity Grouping & Squint Eye-Stop Count** — Cluster related items into `≤ 3–5` visual groups (`margin-top >= 2x margin-bottom`), strip `http://` / `Tel:` prefixes, and use CamelCase URLs (`www.MoonstoneCatchers.com`).
- `M-012`: **Hard-Edge Alignment & Anti-Centering Default** — *"Nothing should be placed on the page arbitrarily."* Lock text and images to a single flush-left/right `"hard vertical edge"` and push `"trapped white space"` outward.
- `M-013`: **Repetitive Visual Anchors & Bold Contrast ("Don't Be a Wimp")** — *"If two items are not exactly the same, then make them different. Really different. Don't be a wimp."*
- `M-014` **Color wheel, tonal contrast and warm–cool color roles.** Use temperature and value to guide attention. A small warm accent can lead on cool ground, and a warm field can carry a brand.
- `M-015` **Type roles and contrast.** Use size, weight and form to distinguish roles. Two sans serifs can work when each has a clear job.
- `M-016`: **Baseline Modular Grid & Progressive Margin Ratios (`1 : 1.5 : 2 : 3`)** — `7 to 10 words per line`, grid fields derived from integer multiples of body line-height (`55 lines = 4 × 13 + 3`), and image edges flush with cap-height and baseline.
- `M-017`: **8/20/32-Field Modular Matrix, Wide + Macro Scale Pairing & `20%–35%` Empty Fields** — Pair spanning context shots with `1×1` macro detail crops while leaving `20%–35%` contiguous empty fields.
- `M-018`: **Color Field Anti-Cramping Rule (Outset Box or Inset Copy)** — Per *"Coloured areas in the grid system"* (Figs. 1–4), never match a colored box flush to text bounds; bleed the color box outward (Fig. 2) or inset copy by `>= 1` line-height (Fig. 3).
- `M-019`: **Product-Free Typographic Event Poster & `170 cm` Booth Eye-Level Rule** — Use asymmetric grid typography and `4x–8x` oversized dates/booth numbers as the sole hero of event invitations, and anchor physical booth panels (`220 × 110 cm`) around the **`170 cm` standing eye-level line**.
- `M-020`: **De-Labeling, Spec-Sheet Label Emphasis & Multi-Line Rich Table Cells** — *"Labels are a last resort"*, *"Combine labels and values"* (`"12 left in stock"`), *"When to emphasize a label"* (`"scanning the page for words like 'depth', not '7.6mm'"`), right-aligned tabular numbers, and `6`-to-`4` column rich table cells.
- `M-021`: **Non-Linear Spacing Scale (`≥25%` Jumps), `45–75` Char Measure & Inverse Line-Height** — *"Start with too much white space"*, *"no two values in your scale are ever closer than about 25%"*, `45–75 char` (`20–35em`) measure, *"Baseline, not center"*, and `1.0–1.2` headline line-height.
- `M-022`: **9-Shade HSL Palettes, Perceived-Brightness Hue Rotation & Flipped Contrast** — `100–900` HSL scales, rotating hue `≤20°–30°` toward `60°/180°/300°` bright peaks or `0°/120°/240°` dark valleys, warm/cool saturated greys, and *"Flipping the contrast"* (pale `100` fill + dark `800` text for WCAG `4.5:1`).
- `M-023`: **Top-Light Dual Shadows, Overlapping Layers, Partial Screenshots & Hero Image Contrast** — *"Emulate a light source"*, *"Shadows can have two parts"*, *"Overlap elements to create layers"*, 3-step monochrome `multiply` hero colorizing, partial screenshots, and `1px` inner shadow to *"Prevent background bleed"*.
- `M-024`: **"Don't Make Me Think" Billboard Design 101 & Eliminating the 3 Visual Noises** — *"We don’t read pages. We scan them. We satisfice. We muddle through."*, *"Clarity trumps consistency"*, visual nesting, *"No cursor = no hover = no clue"*, and *"Presume everything is visual noise until proven innocent"* (**Shouting**, **Disorganization**, **Clutter**).
- `M-025`: **Krug's Third Law ("Cut Half, Then Half Again") & the 6-Item "Trunk Test"** — *"Get rid of half the words on each page, then get rid of half of what’s left"* (*"Happy talk must die"*, *"Instructions must die"*, `103` $\rightarrow$ `34 words`), `"You are here"` cues *"twice as prominent"*, active Tab breaking bottom border, and the 6-item **Trunk Test**.
- `M-026`: **Homepage "Big Bang Theory": `50ms` First Impression, 4 Questions & `6–8 Word` Tagline** — Answer *What is this / What do they have / What can I do / Why here*, pair the logo with a `6–8 word` Tagline passing Jakob Nielsen's logo-swap test (*"Real college. Real-world ready."*), and prevent the Homepage *"Tragedy of the Commons"*.
- `M-027`: **"Reservoir of Goodwill" Visual Protection, Candid FAQs vs. `QWWPWAs` & Ear-Scanning Hierarchy** — Refill buyer goodwill with upfront pricing/MOQ/shipping and **Candid FAQs** (never `QWWPWAs` — *Questions We Wish People Would Ask*), and front-load keywords for users *"scanning with their ears"*.
- `M-028` **Outdoor display contrast and viewing distance.** Preview real conditions. Choose a legible cue for viewers in motion and add necessary detail or a tested QR code where people can stop.
- `M-029`: **Feed-Breaking Isolation (Von Restorff) & Aesthetic-Usability Guardrails** — Audit neighboring feed cards first, isolate ONE focal element using color-independent contrast (shape, size, whitespace, framing), and verify that visual polish never masks missing facts or triggers feed ad blindness.
- `M-030`: **Primacy-Recency Anchoring & Peak-End Storyboarding** — Lock primary hooks and CTAs at Slide 1 and the Final Slide, concentrate hard visual proof into ONE mid-sequence Peak, close with a save-worthy summary/CTA End, and eliminate negative frustration peaks first.
- `M-031`: **Unfinished-Loop Tension (Zeigarnik) & Goal-Gradient Progress Cues** — Pair honest open-loop hooks with right-edge visual bleeds or `15%–25%` horizontal Card Peek, mount compact corner progress counters (`1/5`), and front-load an effortless Step 1 for Head-Start momentum.
- `M-032`: **Mobile Touch Targets (Fitts), Progressive Disclosure (Hick) & Native Conventions (Jakob)** — Keep primary CTAs `≥ 44×44px` with `≥ 8–12px` spacing in thumb zones, clear 9:16 social Safe Zones, group `>3–4` options with 1 highlighted recommendation + progressive disclosure, and follow native platform conventions.
- `M-033` **Placement-specific safe zones and early cues.** Verify the official safe zone for the intended placement. TikTok’s cited recall study uses a first-six-second window.
- `M-034` **Feed creative diagnostics.** Check a comparable account-level baseline and test a hypothesis about the visual, copy, audience, or delivery.
- `M-035`: **Physical-Scale Price Ladder & Emotional-Gauge Framing** — Align 3 physical SKU tiers in one monotone shot using real accompanying volume contrast (center-weighting the flagship tier), strip all body copy, and reframe price numerals via 1 sharp situational question (`"HOW MUCH DO YOU LOVE HER?"`).
- `M-036`: **Familiar Visual-Code Subversion (Double-Take), Single Light-Cone & Cross-Category Container Grafting** — Hijack a 0.3s cultural shorthand via either in-scene object flip + single light-cone (`Fnac`) or pouring/packing a commodity product into a higher-markup category container in a diptych with micro price anchors (`IKEA`), paired with a quiet 3–6 word line.
- `M-037`: **Modern Typographic-Wall Poster (`60%` Condensed Display Type, 3-Tier Scale Drop & Corner QR Close)** — For product-free SaaS/services/events, lock a 3-color dark-field + neon-accent + white palette, pack a 6–9 word manifesto into `55%–65%` condensed bold tight-leading type, enforce a stark 3-tier scale drop, and close with bottom-left URL + bottom-right white-bordered QR code (Channel 2 ready).
- `M-038`: **Icon-as-Diagram Sequential State Repetition & Whisper Reveal** — Visualize invisible safety/stabilization/balancing tech by repeating 1 orientation-sensitive symbol across 3–5 horizontal frames (`Imbalance → Correction → Stabilized Normal`) inside `≥80%` negative space with zero display headline and a tiny single-line footer reveal (Channel 2 vector ready).
- `M-039` **Familiar symbols and authentic deformation.** Make a physical change visible using an object the audience recognizes. Adapt the field and text to the viewing context.
- `M-040`: **Engineering Geometric Ray-Path Diagramming for Invisible Physical Fields** — Visualize invisible glare-free diffusion, acoustics, airflow, or heat transfer using verified cross-section `1–2px` geometric ray paths with subtle intersection glows over a clean matte field and wide margins (`M-018`, Channel 2 vector ready).
- `M-041`: **Architectural Corner Neutral Matrix & Two-Tone Color-Block Staging** — Replace sterile white cutouts with uniform cool/warm grey two-wall corner matrices + isolated hero spreads (`HAY`), or stage low-cost spatial depth with a vertical two-tone wall split and hard-vs-soft material contrast (`Montana`).
- `M-042`: **Mishap-Scene Micro Price-Tag Relief & Empty-Product Narrative Framing** — Frame affordable everyday goods without loud promo starbursts via either a real-life mishap scene with a calm micro price tag (`Guilty Pets`) or an empty-product side anchor framing a warm human moment (`Proudly Second Best`).
