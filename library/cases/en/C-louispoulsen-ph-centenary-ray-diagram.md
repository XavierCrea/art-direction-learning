# Louis Poulsen "PH Centenary Poster": Engineering Geometric Ray-Path Diagramming for Invisible Physical Fields

[English Case Index](../../README.en.md) · [简体中文](../C-louispoulsen-ph-centenary-ray-diagram.md) · Invisible Physics Visualization & Channel 2 Vector Teardown

- **Symptom (`when_stuck`)**: A product's core advantage is an invisible physical phenomenon—glare-free light diffusion, acoustic dispersion, airflow routing, or heat dissipation—that cameras cannot capture and cheesy glowing arrows make look like cheap template art.
- **Channels**: Brand Posters (Ad Banner / Print), PDP Mechanism Sections, Technical Catalogs, Channel 2 Precision Vector Rendering.

## What the Source Material Shows (`observations`)

- **Context & Source**: Danish lighting icon **Louis Poulsen** — *PH Centenary Poster* celebrating Poul Henningsen's classic three-shade logarithmic-spiral lamp system (2026).
- **Matte-Black Engineering Ray Diagram**:
  - The poster uses a deep matte-black ground with generous, even perimeter margins (aligning with `M-018` anti-cramping spacing);
  - Centered in the frame is a precision geometric cross-section diagram of the three shades rendered in fine gold and white vector rays showing light bouncing off the logarithmic-spiral curves downward, punctuated by subtle glowing highlights at 6 reflection intersections.
- **Restrained Typographic Framing**: Only a tiny centered white sans-serif `louis poulsen` wordmark sits near the top and a minimal centenary mark near the bottom—zero marketing slogans or spec tables.

## How Copy and Visuals Work Together (`copy_visual_synergy`)

- **The ray geometry proves the engineering claim**: Instead of writing *"glare-free multi-shade diffusion,"* the physical reflection angles explain *why* direct glare is shielded.
- **Minimalist margins preserve diagram clarity**: Pushing the wordmark and mark into wide top/bottom margins keeps the viewer's eye locked on the optical geometry.

## Reusable Visual Move (`reusable_move`)

1. **Translate Invisible Physics into Verified Cross-Section Ray Paths**:
   - Base the diagram on the product's actual optical, acoustic, thermal, or airflow geometry; render fine `1–2px` vector paths over a clean matte-dark or solid-color field with subtle highlights only at real physical intersections—never arbitrary glowing arrows.
2. **Isolate with Generous Margins & Execute via Channel 2 Vector Code**:
   - Center the diagram inside generous perimeter breathing room (`M-018`) and render via **SVG / Python vector code (Channel 2)** so lines remain razor-sharp and physically accurate.

## Asset Prerequisites & Transfer Limits (`asset_prerequisites` & `transfer_limits`)

- **Asset Prerequisite**: Verified product cross-section geometry or real physical mechanism (actual shade curvature, ducting, or acoustic baffle layout).
- **Transfer Limit**: Never invent decorative fake rays or pseudo-scientific waveforms unrelated to real hardware; skip mechanism diagrams on pure impulse-buy price promos.
