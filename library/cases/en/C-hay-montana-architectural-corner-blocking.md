# HAY × Montana: Architectural Corner Neutral Matrix & Two-Tone Color-Block Staging

[English Case Index](../../README.en.md) · [简体中文](../C-hay-montana-architectural-corner-blocking.md) · Multi-SKU Catalog & Low-Cost Spatial Staging Teardown

- **Symptom (`when_stuck`)**: Multi-SKU / multi-color catalogs on flat white cutouts look like lifeless spreadsheets without 3D volume; or teams lack budget for full architectural interior shoots yet refuse fake AI luxury mansions.
- **Channels**: Product Catalogs & Line Sheets, E-Commerce Multi-SKU Grids (PDP), Brand Hero Visuals & Channel 4 Studio Shoot Briefs.

## What the Source Material Shows (`observations`)

- **Source 1 (HAY × Clara von Zweigbergk)**: Danish brand **HAY** *J-Series* catalog spread (art-directed by Clara von Zweigbergk, documented on *It's Nice That*).
  - The left spread arranges 15 chairs in a crisp `3×5` matrix inside **identical two-wall architectural corners** shaded in cool and warm greys instead of flat white cutouts, creating instant depth;
  - The facing spread isolates a single coral-red `J110` armchair centered inside a cool blue-grey corner with clean white margins and a minimal `J110` header.
- **Source 2 (Montana Furniture)**: **Montana** *Colour Becomes the Protagonist* campaign at 3daysofdesign.
  - Instead of cluttered room props, the backdrop splits vertically into two flat architectural planes—deep aubergine purple and muted sage green;
  - In front sits a bright orange `Panton Wire` metal side table beside rumpled oatmeal linen bedding, pairing crisp glossy metal lines against soft tactile fabric.

## How Copy and Visuals Work Together (`copy_visual_synergy`)

- **Architectural planes create depth and hierarchy without visual noise**: HAY's uniform grey corners unify 15 SKUs while isolating one hero color; Montana's two-tone split gives flat surfaces spatial depth so the hero piece commands attention.
- **Minimalist model headers anchor navigation**: Clean model identifiers (`J110`) sit outside the focal area with zero fluff adjectives.

## Reusable Visual Move (`reusable_move`)

1. **Two-Wall Neutral Corner Matrix (HAY Pattern)**:
   - Photograph or composite multi-SKU variants inside identical two-wall neutral corners (subtle cool/warm wall contrast for 3D volume), paired with an isolated hero-color facing spread.
2. **Vertical Two-Tone Wall Split + Material Contrast (Montana Pattern)**:
   - Stage spatial vignettes using a clean vertical two-color architectural split and pair the hero item with one contrasting tactile material (e.g., coated wire metal vs. natural crumpled linen).

## Asset Prerequisites & Transfer Limits (`asset_prerequisites` & `transfer_limits`)

- **Asset Prerequisite**: Consistent camera angle, horizon height, and corner shadow direction across all cells in a multi-SKU matrix; clear value/chroma separation between hero product and split-wall backdrop.
- **Transfer Limit**: Never mix inconsistent camera perspectives within one matrix; avoid two competing neon/high-chroma walls behind a saturated product.
