# TikTok Vertical Creative: Placement-Specific Safe Zones and Early Cues

[English Case Index](../../README.en.md) · [简体中文](../C-tiktok-9x16-safe-zones-and-2s-hook.md)

## When this helps

Captions, interface controls, or crops cover a headline, price, or action. An opening may also need to give people an earlier reason to watch.

## What TikTok documents

- The safe zone for in-feed ads depends on dimensions, caption length, and additional formats. TikTok supplies templates for different layouts, anchor configurations, and reading directions. Device previews can vary.
- TikTok Creative Codes cites research in which about 90% of ad recall impact was captured in the **first six seconds**.

## How to apply it

1. Confirm the platform, placement, caption length, and add-ons. Compare the final placement preview against the relevant official overlay.
2. Use image, sound, or text to give viewers an early reason to stay. Direct product action or a recognizable scene with a later reveal can both serve this purpose.
3. Check the key information with sound off and interface controls visible. Recheck the overlay when you switch placements or platforms.
