## 1. When Stuck (`when_stuck`)
- **Symptom**: A B2B industrial catalog or technical datasheet mixing product photos, CAD dimension drawings, cross-sections, performance curves, and multilingual text looks like a chaotic jigsaw puzzle; or a physical trade-show booth wall / roll-up banner places key specs, QR codes, or selling points in the bottom half of the panel where tables, chairs, and crowds block visitors from seeing them.

---

## 2. What the Source Material Shows (`observations`)
1. **The 30-Field Grid for a German Industrial Burner Catalog (PDF p.66–67)**:
   - To organize a complex engineering brochure containing burner photographs, dimensional CAD line drawings, internal cross-sections, airflow performance curves, and technical copy, the designer constructed a **30-field spread grid (`15 fields per page = 3 columns × 5 rows`; `6 columns / 30 fields` across a two-page spread)**.
   - **Cover-to-Spread Continuity**: The cover uses 3 horizontal colored bars indicating product divisions locked strictly to grid rows `2, 3, and 4`. Inside, regardless of how complex the engineering schematics and performance charts become, every diagram frame, photo bottom edge, and text column snaps into the `15-field` modular matrix while preserving a consistent top whitespace band across every spread.
2. **Three-Dimensional Exhibition Space Grids & the `170 cm` Standing Eye-Level Rule (*"Grid systems in three-dimensional design"*, PDF p.71–76)**:
   - Unfolding the walls, floor, and ceiling of an exhibition booth into uniform modular grid fields (PDF p.71): each standard vertical display panel measures **`220 cm` high × `110 cm` wide** and is subdivided into a **`2-column × 8-row` (`16-field`)** modular grid.
   - **Anchoring to the `170 cm` Human Eye-Level Line (PDF p.71)**: The elevation diagram explicitly marks the standing visitor's eye level at **`170 cm` above the floor**. All headlines, hero visuals, and body texts are concentrated in the upper-middle golden viewing zone (**grid rows `3–6`**, roughly `100 cm–190 cm` off the ground). The **bottom 2 grid rows** (knee-to-shin height) and the top row are left completely empty.
   - **Traveling Exhibitions & Railway Coach Displays (PDF p.73–76)**: Whether in open exhibition halls or narrow corridors, keeping all reading matter in the upper-middle band around `170 cm` allows visitors to scan comfortably without stooping or peering around furniture.

## 3. How Copy and Visuals Work Together (`copy_visual_synergy`)
- **In B2B technical catalogs**: A `3-column × 5-row` (`15-field`) single-page grid locks *"left-column principles & specs + center-column CAD dimensional drawings + right-column photos & performance curves"* into the same horizontal band, letting engineers cross-verify text and schematics with zero eye-hunting.
- **On trade-show booth walls and roll-up banners**: The `170 cm` eye-level band carries core value propositions, model numbers, and hero graphics; the top header zone carries long-range booth numbers/logos; and the lower `25%` stays clear of tables and crowds.

---

## 4. Reusable Visual Move (`reusable_move`)
1. **Use a `3 × 5` (`15-Field`) Modular Matrix for Multi-Diagram B2B Spec Pages**: Dedicate column 1 to category headings and key spec summaries, and merge columns 2–3 into `2×2` or `2×3` field blocks to house CAD line drawings, wiring diagrams, and performance curves with unified frame alignment.
2. **Enforce the `170 cm` Eye-Level Rule (`Top-Heavy / Bottom-Clear`) on Physical Booths & Banners**: When designing trade-show backwalls, roll-up banners, or vertical display posters, lock all core headlines, booth numbers, QR codes, and key visuals inside the upper-middle band (`15%–65%` down from the top edge), leaving the bottom `25%` as clean background color or non-essential pattern.

---

## 5. Asset Prerequisites & Transfer Limits (`asset_prerequisites` & `transfer_limits`)
- **Asset Prerequisite**: Vector CAD dimensional/wiring drawings with standardized stroke weights and font sizes for catalogs; verified physical panel dimensions (`110 × 220 cm` or `80 × 200 cm`) and furniture height for booth graphics.
- **Transfer Limit**: Mobile online long-scroll images are scrolled on a handheld screen and are not blocked by physical tables; however, for any printed roll-up banner, booth wall, or mounted poster, **never** place critical conversion copy or scan-required QR codes in the bottom `25%`.
