## 1. Triggering Symptoms (`when_stuck`)

- `outdoor billboard visual noise`: Expensive shopping mall facade LED screens or trade-show hero backdrops get swallowed by surrounding neon restaurant signs because they are treated like spec-heavy flyers;
- `dirty landscape gradient on outdoor LED`: Even with few words, an outdoor billboard looks muddy and cramped because a complex landscape background (mountains, forests, dark roads) turns into grey visual noise on street-level LED panels and clashes with tight, top-heavy headlines;
- `split screen fragmentation`: L-shaped corner screens or dual street screens feel chopped into disconnected pieces (e.g., slapping a harsh white logo patch onto a dark photo or repeating the exact same headline on both screens);
- `zero social UGC sharing from outdoor ads`: Passersby never photograph or share the billboard because nobody poses in front of a discount price tag or a wall of fine-print disclaimers.

## 3. Six-Dimensional Visual & Strategic Analysis (`analysis`)

1. **Environmental Denoising ("Pure Color Field" vs. "Muddy Landscape KV")**:
   In Steve Krug's *Don't Make Me Think* (`M-024`), *"When everything on a page is clamoring for your attention, the effect can be overwhelming."* A bustling commercial street is already packed with red/yellow neon signs and architectural lines. Putting a multi-tone mountain/forest landscape KV (Voyah, XPeng) onto a building facade adds more micro-clutter. Flooding `>=75%` of the facade with a single receding cool hue (**Capri Blue**, `M-014`) slices cleanly through the warm red/yellow street noise and gives the eye instant relief.
2. **Typographic Breathing Room ("Restrained Measure" vs. "Top-Heavy Industrial Shouting")**:
   XPeng X9 kept its word count low (`15` characters), yet the headline spans nearly the entire width of the screen, hugs the top bezel, and straddles the busy boundary between blue sky and orange trees. Xiaomi's Changsha screen sets `"一种让时间变慢的蓝"` at a modest scale with generous letter-spacing inside an `80%+` pure blue field—proving that on a 100-square-meter screen, **smaller, calmer typography makes the screen feel larger and more luxurious**.
3. **L-Screen & Dual-Screen Choreography ("Same-Hue Scale Progression" vs. "White-Patch Amputation")**:
   Denza N9 and XPeng X9 sever their screens with jarring solid-white blocks and repeat the same verbal claim on both wings. Xiaomi treats the L-corner and cross-street screens as a **two-shot film sequence in one unified color palette**: Shot 1 (Medium-Wide) shows the car + model + product name (`新一代 SU7`); Shot 2 (Close-Up / Mood Field) shows Shu Qi's calm gaze or a 7-character sensory line.
4. **Awareness Stage & Costly Signaling**:
   Because `小米SU7` already enjoys universal Stage-1 awareness, omitting specs and prices on prime real estate acts as an economic **costly signal**: only brands desperate for immediate clearance cram discount numbers (`16.99万起`) onto a landmark facade; leaving 80% of a million-yuan screen pure Capri Blue signals confidence and luxury stature.
5. **UGC-Ready Backdrop Framing (Turning Media Spend into Organic Social Reach)**:
   Nobody takes a selfie in front of a financing table or a disclaimer footnote. By framing the billboard as a clean fashion portrait + a borrowable holiday mood caption (`"一种让时间变慢的蓝"` / `"五一假期 一起慢下来 享受好时光"`), passersby use the billboard as a photography backdrop on Xiaohongshu (`#蓝色慢游节`).
6. **100m $\rightarrow$ 10m $\rightarrow$ 1m Touchpoint Relay**:
   The exterior screen (`100m`) is only responsible for stopping the eye and sparking curiosity; the ground-floor mall atrium showroom (`1m`) houses the physical Capri Blue SU7 and spec sheets (`LiDAR`, `700 TOPS`, `2200MPa`) that close the sale.

## 4. Image-Copy Synergy (`copy_visual_synergy`)

- **Visuals**: Carry `90%` of the environmental separation and emotional positioning via a full-bleed monochrome Capri Blue color field and relaxed human eye contact.
- **Copy**: Capped at `5–9` Chinese characters (`新一代 SU7` or `一种让时间变慢的蓝`), with zero spec bullets, zero price tags, zero QR codes, and zero bottom footnotes.

---

## 5. Reusable Visual Move (`reusable_move_en`)

When designing large-format visuals for high-noise physical environments (shopping mall facade LEDs, trade-show main-aisle backdrops, booth headers):
1. **Color-Field Denoising**: Strip out busy landscape backgrounds (mountains, forests, multi-tone roads) that turn muddy on outdoor LEDs; flood **`>= 75%` of the canvas with a single high-purity brand Color Field** that cuts through surrounding warm/neon street clutter;
2. **Same-Hue Multi-Screen Progression**: Never chop a wide or L-shaped corner screen with a jarring white logo patch; flow the same color field across the corner and assign **Wide Product Context (`<= 5 words`)** to one panel and **Close-Up Human/Mood Anchor (`<= 8 words`)** to the other;
3. **Restrained Type & Zero Fine Print**: Keep headline width to `<= 60%` of the canvas with `>= 75%` open color-field breathing room, never straddle text across background horizon lines, and purge all unreadable bottom fine print, price grids, and QR codes;
4. **100m-to-1m Touchpoint Relay**: Design the `100m` exterior screen as a clean, shareable social backdrop (`UGC-Ready Backdrop`), deferring dense engineering specs to the `1m` booth catalog, spec sheet, or PDP.

## 6. Asset Prerequisites & Transfer Limits (`asset_prerequisites` & `transfer_limits`)

- **Asset Prerequisites**:
  1. A distinctive, high-purity brand color identity and studio-grade portrait/product photography;
  2. An immediate physical or digital handoff touchpoint (e.g., a ground-floor mall showroom or an on-site trade-show booth within walking distance) where buyers can inspect the physical product and engineering specs.
- **Transfer Limits (Critical Guardrails)**:
  1. **Never omit the product for low-awareness brands in general traffic**: Xiaomi can show only Shu Qi + the `MI` logo on one screen because `SU7` is already a household name and `"Shu Qi = SU7"` is a nationwide homophone. A **low-awareness startup or unfamiliar B2B brand** advertising in general public spaces must **never** copy "celebrity/mood-only without the product"—it must show the product in action + a clear category descriptor (`M-004` / `M-026`), or the budget will be wasted on a cryptic riddle;
  2. **Do not apply zero-spec minimalism to standalone direct-response sheets**: If a single flyer or standalone PDP must close the sale without a showroom relay, concrete proof and specs remain mandatory.
