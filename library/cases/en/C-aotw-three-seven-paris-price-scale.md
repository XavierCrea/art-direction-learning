# Three Seven Paris "How much do you love her?": Physical-Scale Price Ladder & Emotional-Gauge Framing

[English Case Index](../README.en.md) · [简体中文](../C-aotw-three-seven-paris-price-scale.md) · OOH Poster & High-Ticket Physical Price Anchoring Teardown

- **Symptom (`when_stuck`)**: High-ticket gifts, tiered bundles, or multi-size physical SKUs look cold when lined up with plain price tags—causing shoppers to default to the cheapest option—while long craftsmanship copy goes unread.
- **Channels**: Street / Mall OOH Posters, Social Feed Static Covers, Tiered Gift / SKU Comparison Images (PDP / Ad Banner).

## What the Source Material Shows (`observations`)

- **Context & Source**: Parisian handmade ceramic brand **Three Seven Paris** (Agency: The Refreshment Club, February 2023, street-pasted outdoor posters in Paris, 3 photographic assets).
- **Physical Scale & Visual Metaphor**: Three speckled handmade stoneware vases stand side-by-side on a pale off-white surface, each holding white roses scaled directly to its size and price:
  - A medium cylindrical vase with a modest cluster of roses labeled **`220€`**;
  - A large wide vase overflowing with a lush bouquet labeled **`390€`**;
  - A tiny bud vase holding a single solitary rose labeled **`70€`**.
- **Typography & Layout**:
  - Tiny tracked uppercase store address at the top edge (`69 RUE DES SAINTS-PÈRES 75006`);
  - Dominant thin high-contrast serif all-caps headline across the upper third: **`HOW MUCH DO YOU LOVE HER?`**;
  - Clean serif price numerals (`220€`, `390€`, `70€`) placed directly beside each vase with **zero body copy and zero feature bullets**;
  - Ink-black minimalist brand mark ("3" hand gesture + calligraphic "7") and URL centered at the bottom.
- **Color Field & Contrast**: Editorial warm-stone/off-white monotone surface contrasting sharply against crisp black serif type and logo.

## How Copy and Visuals Work Together (`copy_visual_synergy`)

- **The headline reframes passive price tags into an instant emotional gauge**: Asking *"HOW MUCH DO YOU LOVE HER?"* turns prices for the ceramic vases themselves into a playful, unmistakable punchline about romantic generosity.
- **Physical bouquet volume justifies the price gap in 0.5 seconds**: Contrasting a single stem (`70€`) against an overflowing centerpiece (`390€`) lets viewers self-anchor upward without a single sentence explaining ceramic craftsmanship.

## Reusable Visual Move (`reusable_move`)

1. **Replace Dry Tier Tables with a Same-Frame Physical Volume Ladder**: Photograph entry, core, and flagship physical SKUs side-by-side in one frame, using real accompanying contents or outcome scale (bouquet fullness, bundled kit volume, runtime/capacity) to make tier differences physically unmistakable.
2. **Reframe Price Numerals with 1 Sharp Situational Question**: Strip all explanatory body copy and crown the lineup with a single short question that turns the price numbers themselves into the punchline.
3. **Use Center-Weighted Tier Ordering**: Rather than strictly ascending small-to-large, anchor the flagship high-volume tier (`390€`) in the visual center flanked by lower tiers (`220€` / `70€`) to pull the eye toward the hero tier.

## Asset Prerequisites & Transfer Limits (`asset_prerequisites` & `transfer_limits`)

- **Asset Prerequisite**: Requires genuine same-angle, same-lighting multi-SKU photography with truthful retail pricing.
- **Transfer Limit**: Relies on understated wit and editorial restraint—never cross into guilt-tripping or tacky status-shaming copy; unsuitable for purely rational industrial procurement where engineers need objective spec tables rather than emotional framing.
