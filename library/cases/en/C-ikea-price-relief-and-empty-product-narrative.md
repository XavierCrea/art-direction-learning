# IKEA "Guilty Pets" × "Proudly Second Best": Mishap-Scene Micro Price-Tag Relief & Empty-Product Framing

[English Case Index](../../README.en.md) · [简体中文](../C-ikea-price-relief-and-empty-product-narrative.md) · Affordable-Goods Trust & Narrative Framing Teardown

- **Symptom (`when_stuck`)**: Budget-friendly everyday products or consumables want to highlight affordability and warmth, yet standard "Lowest Price / Flash Sale" starbursts immediately cheapen brand perception.
- **Channels**: Social Feed Posters, OOH & Retail Print Posters, Brand Story Hero Visuals.

## What the Source Material Shows (`observations`)

- **Source 1 (IKEA *Guilty Pets*, Agency: Memac Ogilvy)**:
  - High-angle shot of a kitchen corner showing a shattered ceramic coffee mug in a puddle of spilled coffee next to a grey British Shorthair cat staring innocently up at the camera;
  - Floating cleanly above the broken shards is minimal white IKEA typography: `FÄRGRIK Mug Dhs 3`, anchored at bottom-right by the calm line `Don't worry, you can afford it.` and the small IKEA mark.
- **Source 2 (IKEA *Proudly Second Best*, Agency: INGO Stockholm / David Miami)**:
  - Warm domestic photograph of a bearded father holding his toddler on his lap and spoon-feeding him, while a white IKEA `ANTILOP` highchair stands **completely empty** on the left;
  - Beside the empty chair sits `ANTILOP Highchair Dhs 99`, paired at bottom-right with the quiet tagline `Proudly Second Best`.

## How Copy and Visuals Work Together (`copy_visual_synergy`)

- **Authentic domestic moments carry emotion while micro price tags anchor commerce**: In *Guilty Pets*, the broken mug + guilty cat trigger instant empathy while `Dhs 3` + `Don't worry, you can afford it.` turns mishap anxiety into proof of carefree affordability. In *Proudly Second Best*, leaving the product empty lets human warmth take center stage, earning disproportionate goodwill.
- **Zero starbursts or loud discount typography**: Clean, modest sans-serif product-and-price tags replace red/yellow promo stickers.

## Reusable Visual Move (`reusable_move`)

1. **Mishap-Scene Micro Price-Tag Relief**:
   - For affordable, replaceable everyday goods (mugs, pet items, kids' tableware, consumables), photograph a relatable real-life mishap, float a clean minimal price tag directly above the object, and close with one reassuring line.
2. **Empty-Product Narrative Framing**:
   - Leave the hero product empty and clearly identifiable on the side with its price anchor while letting a higher-order human moment occupy the focal center, paired with a humble whisper tagline (reinforcing `M-027`).

## Asset Prerequisites & Transfer Limits (`asset_prerequisites` & `transfer_limits`)

- **Asset Prerequisite**: Genuine, unforced domestic photography (natural pet/human expressions, credible physics) and accurate real-world low-barrier pricing.
- **Transfer Limit**: Never apply "breakable/replaceable" mishap humor to high-ticket durables, precision instruments, or safety gear where breakage signals poor quality; keep the product clearly identifiable.
