# Muserk "Get F'in Paid": Modern Typographic-Wall Poster, 3-Tier Scale Drop & Corner QR Close

[English Case Index](../README.en.md) · [简体中文](../C-aotw-muserk-typographic-wall-poster.md) · Product-Free Poster & Channel-2 Exact Rendering Teardown

- **Symptom (`when_stuck`)**: Promoting an intangible service, SaaS platform, event, or advocacy message with no physical hardware to photograph—where generic 3D blobs or stock handshake photos look hollow and fail to stop street or social-feed traffic.
- **Channels**: Street / Expo Typographic Posters (OOH), Social Big-Type Cover Cards, Product-Free SaaS / Service Ads—ideal for **Channel 2 (Python PIL / SVG exact rendering)**.

## What the Source Material Shows (`observations`)

- **Context & Source**: Music royalty administration tech company **Muserk** (Agency: GS&F, May 2024, Nashville grassroots wheatpaste OOH/print campaign, 3 typographic poster executions).
- **Zero-Photo Typographic-Wall Architecture**:
  - **Background & Hero Visual**: Solid pitch-black field where **~60% of the vertical canvas** is packed edge-to-edge with massive, tight-leading, ultra-condensed heavy all-caps neon magenta/hot-pink display typography, e.g.:
    - `"EVERY SONG. / EVERY ROYALTY. / EVERY F'N CENT."`
    - `"GET WHAT'S F'N / YOURS. TAKE / NOTHING LESS."`
    - `"RELENTLESSLY / SEARCHING FOR / ROYALTIES."`
  - **Strict 3-Tier Scale Drop**:
    1. **Tier 1 (10–30m Street Stop)**: 60%-area condensed hot-pink display block;
    2. **Tier 2 (2–5m Positioning)**: 3-line wide-tracked medium white uppercase subhead directly below (`"WE'RE HELL-BENT ON TRACKING DOWN EVERY ROYALTY YOUR CATALOG GENERATES."`);
    3. **Tier 3 (1m Action Close)**: Tiny white light-weight explainer + bold short URL (`MUSERK.COM/FIXTHEPROBLEM`) at bottom-left, balanced opposite a **white-bordered hot-pink QR code** with center `M` monogram at bottom-right; small white wordmark anchored at top.
- **Palette & Cultural Fit**: Strictly locked to **Black + Electric Magenta + White**, channeling Nashville gig-poster wheatpaste energy native to its songwriter audience.

## How Copy and Visuals Work Together (`copy_visual_synergy`)

- **Typography IS the artwork**: Without any product photography, extreme scale, tight leading, and high-chroma neon-on-black contrast act as a street-level color block from afar and an unmistakable pain-point manifesto up close.
- **Bottom dual-anchor close (`URL left + high-contrast QR right`)**: Converts the emotional jolt into an immediate scan or direct visit with zero visual clutter.

## Reusable Visual Move (`reusable_move`)

1. **Drop Fake Illustrations for Intangible Offers—Make a 60% Condensed Type Wall the Hero**:
   - Distill the sharpest pain point or promise into 6–9 punchy words set in ultra-condensed bold all-caps with tight leading (`0.9–1.05`) spanning 55%–65% of the frame.
2. **Lock a Strict 3-Color Palette & 3-Tier Scale Drop**:
   - Limit color to **`1 pitch-dark background + 1 high-chroma accent hue (hero type & QR only) + 1 crisp white functional text color`**;
   - Enforce stark scale ratios: `Hero Type (100%) → Tracked Subhead (22%–28%) → Footer URL/Copy (10%–14%)`.
3. **Close with Corner URL + High-Contrast White-Bordered QR Code**:
   - Pair directly with Channel 2 (Python PIL / SVG + `zxingcpp` QR verification) for razor-sharp grid alignment and 100% scannability.

## Asset Prerequisites & Transfer Limits (`asset_prerequisites` & `transfer_limits`)

- **Asset Prerequisite**: Requires a genuinely sharp, compressed 6–9 word manifesto—blowing up vague corporate fluff to 60% of the canvas only magnifies its emptiness.
- **Transfer Limit**: Never cram 20–30 word paragraphs into a giant display wall; profanity/rebellious tone must match audience culture and platform rules (for serious B2B/industrial posters, retain the exact dark-field + condensed-giant-type + 3-tier scale + corner QR skeleton using hard metrics or concrete promises).
